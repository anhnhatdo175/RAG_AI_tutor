# Volume Cylinder (intermediate)

## Learning objective
Build reliable understanding of volume cylinder at the intermediate level.

## Level guidance
Solve a two-stage variation and compare the result with an estimate.

## Worked example
With radius 3 and height 5, V = pi × 3² × 5 = 45 pi cubic units.

quantities, and preserve the meaning of the units or set relationships.
