# Venn Diagram (beginner)

<!-- content_type: worked_example -->
## Learning objective
Build reliable understanding of venn diagram at the beginner level.

## Level guidance
Use a worked example, name each quantity, and check units after every step.

## Worked example
If 12 students like A, 9 like B, and 4 like both, then the union has 12 + 9 - 4 = 17 students.

## Why the method works
Start from the definition of venn diagram, substitute only known
quantities, and preserve the meaning of the units or set relationships.
Check the result against the definition and a simple example.


## Practice prompt
Draw two sets with 8 elements in A, 6 in B, and 3 in both. Find the union.

## Retrieval and tutoring notes
- Explain the meaning of each variable before calculating.
- Check the result against the definition and a simple example.
- Ask the learner to show intermediate steps rather than returning only a number.
- If the learner makes an arithmetic error, revisit the corresponding step without
  changing the target skill.
