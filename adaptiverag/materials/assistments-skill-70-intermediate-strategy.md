# Percent Of (intermediate)

<!-- content_type: strategy -->
## Learning objective
Build reliable understanding of percent of at the intermediate level.

## Level guidance
Solve a two-stage variation and compare the result with an estimate.

## Problem-solving strategy
1. Restate what the question asks.
2. Mark the known quantities and the unknown.
3. Select the definition or formula: Find a percentage of a quantity by converting the percent to a decimal or fraction and multiplying.
4. Calculate and verify the result.

## Strategy reminders
- Translate percent language before choosing an operation.
- Use a benchmark such as 10%, 1%, or 50% to estimate.


## Practice prompt
Calculate 15% of 60 and explain why the answer is smaller than 60.

## Retrieval and tutoring notes
- Explain the meaning of each variable before calculating.
- Check the result against the definition and a simple example.
- Ask the learner to show intermediate steps rather than returning only a number.
- If the learner makes an arithmetic error, revisit the corresponding step without
  changing the target skill.
